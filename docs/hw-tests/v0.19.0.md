# v0.19.0: CTCSS and DCS

FM channels use their codeplug tones both ways. On receive the speaker
follows the channel's squelch mode (Channel menu: Sq type): on any
carrier, or on the channel's receive tone alone. A tone counts as heard
after 5 polls of 10 ms and as gone after 15 (CTCSS) or 90 (DCS). Squelch
level 0 forces only the carrier open, so a channel that waits for its tone
stays quiet. On transmit the radio sends the channel's transmit tone,
CTCSS or DCS, normal or inverted. Any carrier keeps battery save off, so
the tone detector has time to lock. The console sets either tone for the
session: `fm tone tx|rx off`, `fm tone tx|rx <0.1 Hz>` (for example 885)
or `fm tone tx|rx d<octal>` with `i` for inverted (for example d023i).
Menu values that were cut short now fit.

## Before you start

- An FM channel with a signal you can trigger, such as a repeater, and
  the tone that signal carries, if any.
- A second station or a receiver that decodes CTCSS and DCS, for the
  transmit steps.
- The log port open, to follow the `fm:` lines.

## Steps

| # | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Flash; watch the start screen and log | "DeltaMM 0.19.0"; no `crash:` line |
| 2 | Channel without a receive tone; trigger the signal | The squelch opens as before; the log says `opens on the carrier` |
| 3 | `fm tone rx` with the tone the signal carries; trigger it | Log `opens on CTCSS ...`; `squelch open, tone heard` |
| 4 | `fm tone rx` with another tone; trigger the signal | The speaker stays quiet; `fm status` shows carrier yes, tone not heard |
| 5 | With a receive tone set, `fm sq 0` | No hiss: the speaker stays quiet until the tone is heard |
| 6 | `fm tone rx off`, `fm sq 0` | Hiss: squelch 0 opens on the carrier as before |
| 7 | Leave the radio until battery save runs, with the matching receive tone set; trigger the signal | Battery save ends at once; `squelch open, tone heard` |
| 8 | `fm tone tx 885`, PTT | Log `transmitting ... CTCSS 88.5 Hz`; the other station decodes 88.5 Hz |
| 9 | `fm tone tx d023`, PTT | Log `DCS 023N`; the other station decodes DCS 023 |
| 10 | `fm tone tx d023i`, PTT | Log `DCS 023I`; the other station decodes inverted DCS 023 |
| 11 | Pick a codeplug channel with tones and squelch mode on the tone | The log shows `opens on` its receive tone; PTT sends its transmit tone |
| 12 | Pick a channel with a receive tone and squelch mode on carrier | Log `opens on the carrier`; a signal without the tone, such as a repeater's CW identifier, opens the squelch |
| 13 | P2: Sq type Tone, then Carrier | The log shows `opens on` the tone after Tone and `the carrier` after Carrier; the value follows in the list |
| 14 | Menu: Battery, Battery save; Keys, Lock covers | Choices `Off`, `On 150 ms`, `Fast wake 70 ms`; `All`, `Keys+PTT`, `Keypad`, `Side+PTT`; nothing cut off |
| 15 | Read the latest uptime line | Every task above 50 unused stack words |
