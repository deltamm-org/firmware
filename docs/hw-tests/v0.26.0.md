# v0.26.0: settings kept on the radio, grouped menu, home screen

Settings changed on the radio are kept in the codeplug. The menu is
grouped by use, with large value screens. The standby screen is a home
screen whose rows are set in the menu. Each part has its own steps and
results below; they were run on images that held these and other
branches before they were merged.

## Settings storage

Settings changed on the radio are written to the codeplug after a quiet
time of 3 s, and hold after a restart (`docs/settings-storage.md`). The
radio never writes while the PC programs it.

This is the first image that writes the codeplug by itself. Steps 1-6,
11 and 13-16 run from the console and the PC. Steps 7-9, 12 and the
screen and menu checks of step 10 need the radio's keys, knob and
screen.

### Before you start

- The log port open; the data port free for `dmrconf`
  (`dmm-radio ports` names both).
- `dmrconf` 0.15.1 or later. In the commands below `<data>` is the data
  port's device.
- A codeplug backup from before this image:
  `dmrconf read -D <data> -R d878uv2 before.yaml` and
  `dmrconf read -D <data> -R d878uv2 before.dfu`.
- The radio on a memory channel, not the VFO.

### Undo

- `dmrconf write -D <data> -R d878uv2 before.yaml` puts the codeplug's
  channels and settings back. Battery save and the zones' last channels
  are not part of what `dmrconf` writes: set them back in the menu or
  with `save <0-2>` if they differ.
- A full byte-for-byte restore: write `before.dfu` back with the data
  link's write command, block by block.

### If something is off

- `settings not kept: the codeplug write failed`: send `settings` and
  `storage`, and report both lines. The values are tried twice more,
  then dropped; the codeplug keeps its old values.
- A setting back at its old value after step 6 or 10: report which one
  and the `codeplug:` line before and after the restart.
- Step 11 read fails or hangs: report the `dmrconf` output and the log
  around it.
- `diff` shows differences in step 15: report the lines. Differences in
  fields the radio changed in steps 7-9 between steps 13 and 15 would
  mean a write slipped in during step 14.

### Report back

- The `codeplug:` lines of steps 1, 9, 10 and 14.
- The ui task's unused stack words from step 16.
- Whether the screen, the menu and the channel matched after step 10.

## Menu: groups by use, large value screens, values kept

The menu lists what is used while operating first: Channel (squelch,
squelch type, power), Roaming (on or off, status, roaming zone), GPS
(on or off, status, satellite systems) and Display (brightness, light
time, help text). Settings holds the setup in groups of its own: Home
screen (see Home screen below), Keys (what the lock covers, auto lock),
Battery (battery save) and Clock (time zone). Tools opens the log and
shows the version; a debug build also has the key test there.

A list shows a title band with where it is and the place in it (`2/5`),
five rows, a scroll bar when there are more, a panel of two lines about
the chosen row and a hint bar. Menu on a row opens a group, a screen or
the row's value screen; a digit from 1 to 9 opens the row at that place.
A value screen shows its choices in one font, the largest its longest
choice fits:

- up to four choices as large buttons, the one in use starred;
- more as one large value between arrows, its place on a scale, and the
  value in use under it; Up raises it, Down lowers it;
- choices with notes (Battery save) as a list.

The squelch, squelch type, brightness and time zone take effect as soon
as they are chosen, so the change is heard or seen at once; Menu keeps
the value, Exit puts back the one in use before. Other values take
effect on Menu, and Exit leaves them as they were. Exit in a list goes
back one level, onto the row it came from. P2 opens Channel straight
from the standby screen; Exit or a value set goes back there. The zone
list (P1) and the roaming status look like the menu's lists; the
roaming status names the repeaters heard and points at the one in use.
On the standby screen Up and Down step the zone, or what Settings >
Home screen > Up/Down sets (see Home screen below); the channel knob steps
the channel. Every screen is drawn in place: nothing blanks before it is
redrawn.

Values set in the menu are kept in the codeplug
(`docs/settings-storage.md`): the squelch, light, key lock, battery
save, GPS, satellites, time zone, roaming on or off and roaming zone with
the general settings, power and squelch type with the channel.

keep the log port open for the log lines named under Expect and for the
console commands. Keys are named as on the radio (Menu, Exit, Up,
Down, P1, P2, the digits); "hold" means holding the key until the screen
changes. "Knob 1 on" turns the channel knob one click clockwise, "knob 2
back" two clicks anticlockwise. Each step starts where the one before
ended. Steps 31 and 32 need an FM channel and a roaming DMR channel.

## Home screen: rows set in the menu

The standby screen is the home screen, in rows under the status bar:

- the top line (left) and the clock (right);
- the large name;
- a row on a panel;
- the power, with a reading next to it;
- the bottom row;
- the key hints; `#` names the mode it switches to, `# VFO` on a memory
  channel and `# MEM` in the VFO.

Menu > Settings > Home screen, first in Settings, sets each row:

| Row | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| Preset | Detailed, Simple, Roaming; `Own` once a row is changed by itself |
| Top line | Talkgroup (`TG 9`, `CHANNEL` without a contact), Zone, Number (`CH 2/5`), None |
| Clock | On, Off |
| Large name | Channel, Contact (the contact's name), Zone |
| Middle row | Frequency (`MHz`, slot and colour code, or `FM` and the bandwidth), Talkgroup (the contact and a name), Signal (meter) |
| Next to power | Voltage (`BAT 7.80V`), Volume (`VOL 8`), Signal (`SIG -90`), None (nothing) |
| Bottom row | Details, Last caller, Position, None |
| Up/Down | Zone (Up the next zone in use, Down the one before, wrapping), Channel (as the channel knob, in the VFO too), Off |

Up/Down is not part of a preset; Zone is its default. A zone step lands
on the channel last selected in that zone as the codeplug keeps it (the
zone list does the same) and does nothing in the VFO.

Detailed is the default. Simple: the clock, the channel's name large,
its frequency in the middle row and the power, nothing else (no top
line, nothing next to the power, no bottom row or its rule). Roaming:
the signal meter in the middle row. With the bottom row None, a
receive-only channel still shows `RX ONLY` there, and the rule above it.

Details are the repeater a roaming channel works through (`via 2 NAME`,
counted from 1 as `roam` counts), an FM channel's tones and offset, or
else the zone and the channel's place in it (the channel's name instead
when something else is large), also on an FM simplex channel without
tones.

On a roaming channel the Frequency row shows the repeater in use,
`Searching` during a search and `No repeater` while the repeater in use
is lost.

The signal meter has 10 bars, one per 4 dB from -105 dBm (none) to -65
dBm (all), and the level in dBm. The level is read only while a carrier
is heard and shown for 2 s after; on a roaming channel the repeater's
last level is shown while it is current (2 minutes). Otherwise, and
after a restart until the first signal, it shows `-` and no bars.

The status bar keeps its TX, RX, LOCK and GPS marks; the battery is a
small icon (19 by 7 pixels) at the right end, filled by its level, red
when low. Only rows whose shown value
changes are drawn again: the clock each minute, the voltage when its
digits change, the volume, the signal level, the last call's age, the
position.

The layout is kept in the preferences record and survives a restart and
a codeplug write from QDMR. A record from an earlier build gives the
default layout.

### Before

- A DMR channel whose contact is a talkgroup with a name other than the
  channel's, a roaming channel, and an FM channel in the codeplug, and
  at least two zones in use.
- A codeplug read with QDMR to undo with: `dmrconf read -D <data> -R
  d878uv2 before.yaml`.
- The log port open for the log lines named under Expect and for the
  console commands. Keys are named as on the key test (Menu, Exit, Up,
  Down); "look" means looking at the radio's screen.

The menu remembers the row last chosen in each group until a restart, so
the way into the group depends on what came before:

- `H`, after a restart: press Menu, Down, Down, Down, Down, Menu, Menu (Menu,
  Settings, Home screen), on Preset.
- `M`, when the menu was last left from the group: press Menu three
  times, back on the row last chosen there.
- `X`: press Exit three times, back to the home screen.
