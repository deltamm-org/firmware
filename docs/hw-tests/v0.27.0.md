# v0.27.0: roaming by signal quality

The repeater in use is strong at 20 dB above the floor or more: no
listening and no move then. Good (6 to 20 dB above) or fair (below 6 dB),
the radio checks the zone's other repeaters during the idle transmissions
of the repeater in use, runs a burst of passes 15 minutes after the last
while good and 10 minutes while fair, and leaves it for one heard twice
and 6 dB better. Heard only below 6 dB for 135 s it is poor: bursts
with gaps growing from 2 to 5 minutes, a move to one heard 6 dB better,
and the screen keeps showing it. Lost with nothing at all heard for an
hour, the gaps grow too. The roam status view shows the state by the same
name, the site search, and each repeater heard with its level while
current.

The level of the repeater in use is the mean of its last three readings,
each the mean of its bursts over 5 s of a transmission (a beacon gives
one, a call one every 5 s), none older than 135 s. Strong ends only
3 dB below the strong level, and fair only 3 dB above the fair level.
With the floor at -100 dBm: strong from -80 dBm until below -83 dBm;
fair below -94 dBm until -91 dBm or more.

Needs a codeplug with a roaming zone of the local network's frequency
pairs, a roaming channel, automatic roaming on; in reach, one repeater at
a strong level and one below it; USB to a laptop for the log.

## Results

Run 2026-11-21 on a D878UVII PLUS, the roaming floor at -100 dBm on both
bands, the radio's own antenna, the log read on a laptop over USB. At
home, where the 70 cm repeater in use beacons every minute and the level
was set by loosening the antenna, and on a drive of about 20 km with two
more repeaters of the zone in reach. A roaming zone of 25 frequency
pairs, then one of 8. Steps 4, 11, 13 and 16 ran on the final image; the
others on this branch before its last four changes (the 135 s limit, the
level shown while current, the burst timer, calls of interest), which do
not touch them.
