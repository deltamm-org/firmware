# v0.27.0: roaming by signal quality

The repeater in use is strong at 20 dB above the floor or more: no
listening and no move then. Good (6 to 20 dB above) or fair (below 6 dB),
the radio checks the zone's other repeaters during the idle transmissions
of the repeater in use, runs a burst of passes 15 minutes after the last
while good and 10 minutes while fair, and leaves it for one heard twice
and 6 dB better. Heard only below 6 dB for 2 minutes it is poor: bursts
with gaps growing from 2 to 5 minutes, a move to one heard 6 dB better,
and the screen keeps showing it. Lost with nothing at all heard for an
hour, the gaps grow too. The roam status view shows the state by the same
name and the site search.

Needs a codeplug with a roaming zone of the local network's frequency
pairs, a roaming channel, automatic roaming on; in reach, one repeater at
a strong level and one below it; USB to a laptop for the log.

## Steps

| # | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Flash; `roam` | the zone's repeater count, automatic on, the repeater in use |
| 2 | On a repeater in use at -80 dBm or more for 15 min | No `roam: check` lines, no passes; Roam status: Strong, no Site search row |
| 3 | `roam use <n>` with a repeater heard below -80 dBm while a much stronger one is in reach | Good or Fair in Roam status; checks during its idle transmissions; a move to the stronger one once heard twice and 6 dB better |
| 4 | On a good repeater in use for 30 min with nothing better in reach | A burst of passes about every 15 min, none during a call or within 10 s of it; Site search with the minutes to the next, then now |
| 5 | A repeater in use heard only below -94 dBm (`roam use <n>`, or the radio shielded) for over 2 min | Roam status: Poor; bursts with gaps of 2, then 4, then 5 min; its calls still play; the screen keeps showing it, no `No repeater` |
| 6 | As in 5, with another repeater heard only a few dB stronger | No move to it; no swinging between the two |
| 7 | As in 5, with a repeater 6 dB or more stronger in reach | A move to it within a few minutes |
| 8 | The repeater in use not heard at all for 2 min after fading out | `No repeater` under the channel name; Roam status: Lost |
| 9 | A call on the repeater in use while good | No check or pass during the call or within 10 s of it; the call is heard |
| 10 | PTT on a roaming channel, idle and during a beacon | As in v0.25.0: a wake-up when idle, none during a beacon |
| 11 | A long run of calls on the repeater in use while its level drops below -94 dBm (the antenna loosened during the calls) | `roam` shows the level easing down over a minute or two, as with beacons; Roam status Fair within about 2 min, then Poor after 2 min below -94 dBm |

## Results

Run 2026-11-14 on a D878UVII PLUS with these changes, then numbered
0.26.0, the roaming floor at -100 dBm on both bands, indoors, the log
read on a laptop over USB. A roaming zone of 8 frequency pairs; in reach
a 70 cm repeater (repeater 1) that beacons every minute, heard at -66 to
-79 dBm, and a 2 m repeater (repeater 2); traffic came from TG 91. Where
a lower level was needed, the radio was shielded. PTT only in step 10;
console commands as in v0.25.0 (`roam use <n>`, `roam forget`, `roam
auto off`, `roam now`).

Steps 3, 5, 8 and 11 and the Roam status of step 4 were run on
2026-11-17 with 0.27.0, on a roaming zone of 25 frequency pairs with the
same kind of pair: a 70 cm repeater that beacons every minute, heard at
-62 to -74 dBm, and a 2 m repeater heard at -85 to -98 dBm when woken.
For steps 5, 8 and 11 the antenna was loosened, and for step 8 taken off;
traffic came from TG 244, and in step 11 from TG 91 after one PTT.
