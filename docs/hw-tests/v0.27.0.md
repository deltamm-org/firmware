# v0.27.0: roaming by signal quality

The repeater in use is strong at 20 dB above the floor or more: no
listening and no move then. Good (6 to 20 dB above) or fair (below 6 dB),
the radio checks the zone's other repeaters during the idle transmissions
of the repeater in use, runs a burst of passes 15 minutes after the last
while good and 10 minutes while fair, and leaves it for one heard twice
and 6 dB better. Heard only below 6 dB for 135 s it is poor: bursts
with gaps growing from 2 to 5 minutes, a move to one heard 6 dB better,
and the screen keeps showing it. Lost with nothing at all heard for an
hour, the gaps grow too. The roam status view shows the state by the same
name, the site search, and each repeater heard with its level while
current.

The level of the repeater in use is the mean of its last three readings,
each the mean of its bursts over 5 s of a transmission (a beacon gives
one, a call one every 5 s), none older than 135 s. Strong ends only
3 dB below the strong level, and fair only 3 dB above the fair level.
With the floor at -100 dBm: strong from -80 dBm until below -83 dBm;
fair below -94 dBm until -91 dBm or more.

Needs a codeplug with a roaming zone of the local network's frequency
pairs, a roaming channel, automatic roaming on; in reach, one repeater at
a strong level and one below it; USB to a laptop for the log.

## Steps

| # | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Flash; `roam` | `DeltaMM 0.27.0`; the zone's repeater count, automatic on, the repeater in use |
| 2 | On a repeater in use at -80 dBm or more for 15 min | No `roam: check` lines, no passes; Roam status: Strong, no Site search row |
| 3 | `roam use <n>` with a repeater heard below -80 dBm while a much stronger one is in reach | Good or Fair in Roam status; checks during its idle transmissions; a move to the stronger one once heard twice and 6 dB better |
| 4 | On a good repeater in use for 30 min with nothing better in reach | A burst of passes about every 15 min, none during a call for us or our own transmission, or within 10 s of one; Site search with the minutes to the next, then now |
| 5 | A repeater in use heard only below -94 dBm (`roam use <n>`, or the antenna loosened) for over 135 s | Roam status: Poor; bursts with gaps of 2, then 4, then 5 min; its calls still play; the screen keeps showing it, no `No repeater` |
| 6 | As in 5, with another repeater heard only a few dB stronger | No move to it; no swinging between the two |
| 7 | As in 5, with a repeater 6 dB or more stronger in reach | A move to it within a few minutes |
| 8 | The repeater in use not heard at all for 135 s after fading out | `No repeater` under the channel name; Roam status: Lost |
| 9 | A call for us, and a short transmission of our own (to a test or echo contact), on the repeater in use while good | No check or pass during either or within 10 s of it; the call is heard |
| 10 | PTT on a roaming channel, idle and during a beacon | As in v0.25.0: a wake-up when idle, none during a beacon |
| 11 | A long run of calls on the repeater in use while its level drops below -94 dBm (the antenna loosened during the calls) | `roam` shows a new level about every 5 s during the calls, reaching the new level within about 15 s; Roam status Fair within about 20 s of the drop, then Poor after 135 s below -94 dBm |
| 12 | A long run of calls on a strong repeater in use, the antenna loosened so that it reads about -81 to -83 dBm | Roam status stays Strong, no checks; loosened further, below -83 dBm: Good within about 15 s, checks start |
| 13 | As in 11, then the antenna tightened so that it reads about -92 to -94 dBm, then -90 dBm or more | Fair stays while at -92 to -94 dBm; Good within about 15 s once at -91 dBm or more; after Poor, no burst of passes starts at once on turning Good |
| 14 | On a repeater in use with beacons only (no calls), level near -80 dBm or -94 dBm, for 15 min | No flipping of Roam status between beacons that differ by a dB or two; the level changes once a beacon, by about a third of the step, also with checks during the beacons |
| 15 | Roam status while a repeater other than the one in use was last heard over 135 s ago, and with beacons a minute apart, one of them missed | The old one shows only its age (`3m`), no level; the repeater in use keeps its level and stays as it was through the missed beacon |
| 16 | Calls for other talkgroups (not in the channel's receive list) on the repeater in use while good, back to back | Checks go on during them, and a due burst of passes starts; none held for 10 s after each |
