# v0.24.2: DMR receive set-up, slot lock, call changes, LEDs

A DMR slot locks only after four good access channel bursts, and every
receive set-up ends the old reception and idles the baseband before the
new one. When the baseband stops taking commands, ten in a row, the
radio restarts it and sets up receive again. A lost slot timing ends
only a call on that slot. A voice call header with another caller or
target starts a new call; link control on another colour code than the
channel's is ignored.

The blue LED lights while DMR is heard, the green one while there is a
carrier on the channel's own frequency. The transceiver's GPIO6 gives
out the squelch signal. Each DMR burst and "timing found" line
shows the receiver level. Console: `dmr level on|off` (a per-minute
level summary), `log debug on|off` (debug lines out of the log queue,
the uptime report now among them), `board usb clock pll|internal`.

## Steps

| # | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Flash | "DeltaMM 0.24.2"; `fm: started, pin register ... -> 9ae2` |
| 2 | Park on a 2 m DMR repeater for a few minutes, the log port read once a minute | Every beacon decoded: `repeater timing found, N dBm`, the bursts with their level and the repeater's colour code |
| 3 | The same on a 70 cm DMR repeater | As step 2 |
| 4 | `dmr level on` for a few minutes on each repeater, then `dmr level off` | One `level over 60 s: low ..., middle ..., high ... dBm` line a minute: high near the beacon's level, middle near the empty channel's; no more lines after off |
| 5 | Watch the LEDs on a DMR repeater channel | Green while the beacon's carrier is on, blue while its timing is found |
| 6 | Listen to a busy talkgroup through a repeater | Each over shows its own caller, also when one follows another with no pause; calls end with their over |
| 7 | 70 retunes between 2 m and 70 cm DMR channels, 0.5 to 1.2 s apart (`dmr rx`) | Every set-up taken; no `commands in a row not taken` warning; beacons still decoded after it |
| 8 | A DMR call through each repeater | The reply decoded and heard to its end; receive back after transmitting |
| 9 | `log debug off`, the log port closed for 20 minutes, then read; `log debug on` | All info lines of the 20 minutes there, no `lines dropped`, no uptime report; with debug on the uptime report every 10 s again |
| 10 | `board usb clock internal`, then `board usb clock pll` | `USB clock internal 48 MHz`, then `USB clock from the PLL`; the console works with both, beacons decoded with both |
| 11 | On a DMR repeater for a few hours | No `commands in a row not taken` warning; after one, if any, receive is back |
