# v0.24.1: DMR receive levels, synthesizer lock, FM levels

Digital channels receive at the level the baseband's demodulator is
calibrated at, one volume step above the FM one. The baseband's DC
offset follows the channel's frequency between the calibration points
instead of the value for the bottom of its range. While receiving, the
radio checks every 100 ms that the transceiver's synthesizer is still
locked, and locks it again if not, with a warning in the log. A retune
within digital receive sends the baseband only the settings that
changed.

FM: the codeplug's FM mic level (1 to 5) sets the transmit gain; level 3
keeps the gain used so far. Channels with an alternative band setting
transmit with a little more voice deviation. Wide channels leave
registers 0x35 and 0x36 as they were.

## Steps

| # | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Flash | "DeltaMM 0.24.1"; `fm: microphone level N of 5`, N the codeplug's FM mic level; no "synthesizer unlocked" warning while idle on a channel |
| 2 | A 2 m DMR repeater, a few minutes between calls | Bursts and calls decoded at least as before at the same place |
| 3 | A 70 cm DMR repeater, the same | As step 2 |
| 4 | FM mic level 3: `fm 44` on a DMR channel, then on an FM channel | `0AFA` on DMR, `09F9` on FM |
| 5 | A DMR call through each repeater, then an FM call | Heard and answered as before; no "synthesizer unlocked" warning after transmitting |
| 6 | Move between DMR channels on both bands for a minute, quickly at times | No "synthesizer unlocked" warning; no refused receive set-up; calls still heard after it |
| 7 | FM mic level 1, then 5 (codeplug write, restart); `fm 44` on an FM channel; an FM call each time | `07F9`, then `0CF9`; the voice clearly quieter at 1 and louder at 5 than at 3 |
| 8 | `fm 35` on a DMR channel, then on a wide FM channel, then on a narrow one | The wide channel reads the same as the DMR one (kept), the narrow one differently; the register reads back the chip's state, not the value written |
| 9 | Into a dummy load: `fm tx 433000`, `ptt 3000`, `fm 59` while it transmits; the same at 433.500 MHz | The upper ten bits 6 higher at 433.000 MHz than at 433.500 MHz |
