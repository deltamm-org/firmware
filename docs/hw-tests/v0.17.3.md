# v0.17.3: Board facts out of shared code

Radio-, chip- and board-specific code moved out of the shared folders.
Calibration, codeplug and data flash are read through model-named
classes, the baseband's codec set-up comes from the board, and each chip
service runs its own console commands. Keys have generic names; the board
gives their on-screen labels. Behaviour should be as in 0.17.2, except
that the log names keys generically.

## Before you start

- 0.17.2 on the radio works as expected, for comparison.
- An FM channel with a station to talk to, and the USB log open.

## Steps

| # | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Flash; watch the start screen and log | "DeltaMM 0.17.3"; log shows `calibration: copy A, battery offset ...` (or copy B) and `codeplug: zone ...` |
| 2 | Look at the battery bars | Same as on 0.17.2 |
| 3 | Turn the volume knob from bottom to top on a busy channel | Silent at the bottom, steps up evenly, as loud at the top as on 0.17.2 |
| 4 | Receive a station | Audio at the same level and quality as on 0.17.2; the log's `squelch open` line ends with RSSI, noise and quality |
| 5 | PTT and talk | The other station hears clear audio at normal loudness |
| 6 | Console `help` | Two extra lines: `FM chip: ...` and `baseband chip: ...` |
| 7 | Console `fm 1f`, then `fm status` | The register's value; then frequency, carrier, speaker, RSSI, noise, quality |
| 8 | While receiving: `fm tune 0`, then `fm tune auto` | Reception drops at 0, comes back at auto (log says "calibrated") |
| 9 | Console `bbtone 1000 500` | A 1 kHz tone as loud as on 0.17.2 |
| 10 | Console `bb packet 20 30` | Log shows a reply with the baseband's product name |
| 11 | Console `codec 1 10 17` | `unknown command` (the old name); `bb codec 1 10 17` works instead |
| 12 | Look at the standby screen's bottom bar | `Menu  P1 Zone  P2 Chan  # VFO` as before |
| 13 | Console `keytest`; press P1, P2, PF1, PF2, PF3, PTT | Boxes labelled as on the radio light up; log says `key Front1 pressed`, `Front2`, `Side1`, `Side2`, `Side3`, `PTT` |
| 14 | Lock (hold *); press P1 and PF1 | Both refused as on 0.17.2 |
