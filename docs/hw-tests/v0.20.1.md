# v0.20.1: no keys before the radio is up

The start screen stays up until the radio's chips are ready, about five
seconds after power-on, with "Please wait..." under the version. Until
then every key and knob turn is refused, so nothing reaches a chip that
is not yet set up and PTT cannot key the transmitter. While a chip
restarts (`bb restart`) keys and PTT are refused with a "Please wait..."
notice. A PTT release always goes through.

## Before you start

- The log port open.

## Steps

| # | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Flash; power on and press keys, turn the knob and press PTT at once | The start screen with "Please wait..."; nothing happens until the standby screen shows; the log has `radio: ready` after `baseband: start took ...`; no transmission |
| 2 | Once the standby screen is up, press keys | They work as before |
| 3 | Console `bb restart`, then press keys and PTT at once | Notice "Please wait..."; log `key refused: the radio is not ready`; nothing is sent |
| 4 | After `radio: ready`, press keys and PTT | They work as before |
