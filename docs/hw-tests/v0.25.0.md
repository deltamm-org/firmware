# v0.25.0: listen-first roaming

Roaming listens first and transmits only for a call or when asked. The
repeater in use is good, weak, quiet (silent after a good level) or lost
(silent after fading out, or not answering a wake-up). While it is not
clearly good the radio listens to the zone's other repeaters: single
checks during its idle bursts, passes in bursts of a minute while it is
quiet or lost, with gaps growing from 2 to 5 minutes while quiet and
staying at 2 while lost. Automatic roaming moves only between calls.
Calls and searches wake a repeater the same way.

Needs a codeplug with a roaming zone of the local network's frequency
pairs, a roaming channel per talkgroup, automatic roaming on, and a side
key set to Roaming; a repeater in use that beacons, and if possible one
that does not; a way to go out of reach of the repeater in use and back,
or to another repeater's area; USB to a laptop for the log.

## Steps

| # | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Flash; `roam` | "DeltaMM 0.25.0"; the zone's repeater count, automatic on, the repeater in use |
| 2 | Stay in reach of the beaconing repeater in use, at a good level, for 15 min | No `roam: check` lines; `roam` shows it good |
| 3 | PTT on a roaming channel while the repeater in use is idle | `repeater wake-up 1: answered`, then the call goes out through it |
| 4 | PTT while it is on the air (a beacon or hang time) | The call goes out without a wake-up |
| 5 | Press the Roaming key | `searching the roaming zone`, the repeater in use tried first; `staying on repeater N` or a move to a stronger one |
| 6 | `roam auto off`; go out of reach of the repeater in use; PTT | `No answer` on the screen, no search; `roam auto on` afterwards |
| 7 | Go out of reach for 10 min or more, without PTT | `No repeater` under the channel name once it has faded; passes of a minute every 2 minutes in the log |
| 8 | Come back in reach, or reach another repeater's area | A move within a few minutes: `moving from repeater N (lost) to repeater M` |
| 9 | Stay by a strong repeater in use that sends no beacons, with little traffic, for 30 min | It stays in use; passes with gaps of 2, then 4, then 5 minutes, never longer |
| 10 | A call on the repeater in use while the radio listens | No pass starts during the call or within 10 s of it; the call is heard |
| 11 | Menu > Channel > Roaming off, then on; Roam zone to another zone | Each applies at once |
| 12 | Read the log of a pass | Each check logs the level read, also on an empty channel |
| 13 | `roam use <n>` with a silent repeater, then `roam forget`; wait, without PTT | Passes in the roaming zone's order, the frequency in use left out; a move to the first repeater heard at the good level |
| 14 | `roam auto off`, `roam forget`, `roam use <n>` with a repeater out of reach that is not first in the roaming zone; press the Roaming key; `roam auto on` afterwards | The repeater in use tried first with a call's wake-ups, then the others in the roaming zone's order with one each; the search ends at the first good one |
