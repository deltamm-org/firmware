# v0.16.0: channels, zones, VFO and menu

The radio starts on the codeplug's channel: the main side's zone and that
zone's last channel, or the VFO. The standby screen shows the zone, the
channel name, frequency, mode, tones, power and offset, with the quick
keys named in the bottom bar. FM channels are tuned from the channel:
receive and transmit frequency, width, CTCSS transmit tone, power and the
codeplug's squelch level and transmit timeout. Digital channels switch
the receiver off for now. The key lock takes its settings from the
codeplug. Nothing is written back to the codeplug.

Keys on the standby screen: channel knob or Up/Down change channel (VFO:
12.5 kHz steps); digits type a frequency (six digits, or fewer then
Menu); # switches between VFO and memory; P1 opens the zone list; P2 the
current channel's settings; Menu opens the menu groups. Exit held cycles
standby, log and key test. (v0.16.1 adds the start screen, menu groups,
P2, the volume display and the plainer style.)

## Before you start

- Antenna or dummy load on: steps 7 and 8 transmit.
- Know which zone and channel factory firmware last had selected, and the
  names of a few channels in it.

## Steps

| # | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Flash; watch the screen after the start screen | Standby screen with the same zone and channel the factory firmware had selected; log `ui: codeplug: zone N "name", M channels` |
| 2 | Compare with the CPS: name, frequency, offset, tone, width, power | All match the CPS |
| 3 | Turn the channel knob both ways; then Up/Down | Channels step and wrap within the zone; the position (n/M) follows; log `fm: receiving ...` for each FM channel |
| 4 | Pick a digital channel | "DMR" in the details; log `digital channel ...: not supported yet` |
| 5 | P1, choose another zone with the knob, Menu | Zone list shows every zone by name; the chosen zone opens on its last channel |
| 6 | Type 1 4 5 4 5 0 | Shows 145.450 as you type, then tunes the VFO to 145.450 MHz |
| 7 | PTT 2 s on the VFO (145.450) | `fm: transmitting 145.450 MHz` at the VFO's power |
| 8 | # back to memory, pick the 2 m repeater channel, PTT | Opens the repeater (its tone and offset come from the channel) |
| 9 | Menu: open Channel, Display and Keys; change each setting. P2: change squelch | Each list shows the current value; the change takes effect at once and the list shows the new value (P2 returns to standby) |
| 9b | Turn the volume knob | VOL n in the status row follows; a volume bar shows for a moment |
| 10 | Hold * | Locks with the codeplug's lock categories; LOCK in the status bar |
| 11 | Unlock; Exit held three times | Log, key test, back to standby |
| 12 | Flash factory V3.06 back; check FM, DMR and the codeplug | Works as before; the zone and channel the factory firmware shows are unchanged |

## Results

v0.16.1 and v0.16.2, 2026-10-21: steps 2 to 11 pass. Step 1 started in
the right zone but on another of its channels (fixed in v0.16.3). In
step 9 the Backlight menu allowed level 0, which darkens the display
