# v0.20.2: no flicker on screen updates

Screen updates no longer blank an area before drawing it again. A volume
step changes only the status bar's volume field and the volume notice's
number and bar; the standby and call screens are redrawn in place, and a
call's running time ticks without blanking its bar.

## Steps

| # | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Flash; turn the volume knob slowly through its range | "DeltaMM 0.20.2"; only the notice's number and bar and the status bar's `VOL` change, nothing blinks |
| 2 | Wait for the volume notice to go | The standby screen comes back without the screen blinking black |
| 3 | Turn the volume, change channel with the knob, turn the volume again | The volume notice comes back whole; no channel text shows through it |
| 4 | Step between a channel with a long name (smaller font) and one with a short name | Nothing of the previous name is left behind |
| 5 | A DMR call | The call screen appears in place; the time ticks without the bottom bar flashing |
