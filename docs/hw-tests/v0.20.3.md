# v0.20.3: activity on the status LEDs

The status LEDs show what the radio does on the air: green while an FM
signal is heard, blue while a DMR call is heard, red while transmitting
(FM or DMR, a repeater wake-up included). The green heartbeat is gone;
with a low battery the red LED blinks when not transmitting.

## Steps

| # | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Flash; watch the LEDs while idle | "DeltaMM 0.20.3"; a short red flash at start-up, then all LEDs off; no green blink |
| 2 | An FM channel with a signal | Green while the speaker is open |
| 3 | A DMR channel with a call | Blue while the call is heard |
| 4 | PTT on FM, then on DMR | Red while transmitting, the DMR repeater wake-up included |
